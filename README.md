# DESKIFY-OFFICE-SUPPLIES-Sales-Customer-Analytics
Power BI Sales &amp; Customer Analytics Dashboard | Deskify Office Supply Co. Developed as part of my 10alytics learning journey, this project focused on transforming Excel-based sales and transaction data into an interactive Power BI business intelligence solution.
The project involved data cleaning, data modeling, Power Query, DAX, and dashboard development to create a centralized reporting solution for analyzing business performance.



I designed a structured data model with Transaction Fact, Customer, Product, Order, Location, and Calendar dimensions, then created dynamic measures to track key KPIs such as Total Revenue, Total Profit, Total Orders, Total Customers, and Profit Margin.

The final solution includes dedicated Product Analysis and Customer Analysis dashboards, providing insights into product profitability, customer segments, regional performance, shipping modes, sub-category performance, and monthly profit trends.



The analysis generated key business insights, including the strong contribution of Office Supplies to overall profitability and the high profitability of the Corporate customer segment.



This project strengthened my ability to move from raw data → data preparation → data modeling → DAX calculations → interactive visualization → actionable business insights.



Tools & Skills: Power BI | Power Query | DAX | Data Modeling | Data Cleaning | Data Visualization | Business Intelligence | Dashboard Design
